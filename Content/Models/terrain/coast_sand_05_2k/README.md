# Coast Sand 05 material

The **Grass Ground** auto-material preset uses this scan for its sand layer.
Select **Terrain Sculpt > Auto material type > Grass Ground** in the level
editor. Custom sand-layer overrides take priority over the preset.

These 2048 x 2048 maps were imported from the user-provided
`C:\Users\Bas\Downloads\coast_sand_05_2k\textures` folder. Color, AO, and
displacement are unchanged copies. The OpenGL normal and roughness EXRs were
converted to 8-bit PNG by clamping linear channels to [0, 1] and rounding to
[0, 255], without gamma conversion or a normal green-channel flip. The preset
uses packaged Content paths and does not depend on the Downloads folder.
