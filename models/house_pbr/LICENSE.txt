Destructible house material textures.

Maps per material (Color + NormalGL + Roughness, AO where available):
  stud_wood.*        <- Wood062_1K-JPG
  cladding_wood.*    <- Planks011_1K-JPG   (cladding albedo uses stud_wood single board)
  roof_tiles.*       <- RoofingTiles002_1K-JPG   (no AO map published)

Suffixes: (base).jpg = color, _normal.jpg = NormalGL, _roughness.jpg = roughness
(sampled as glTF metallic-roughness G channel), _ao.jpg = ambient occlusion
(downloaded; not yet consumed by the shader).
